## 2. What the App does

Apps.SpamMonitor is a free, open-source anti-spam App for Rocket.Chat. It runs inside your own Rocket.Chat instance and helps administrators detect and act on spam behavior (duplicate/fuzzy message matching, rate flooding, cross-channel spam, link spam), configure automated restrictions, and generate scheduled admin reports. See our [Privacy Policy](./PRIVACY.md) for how it handles data.
